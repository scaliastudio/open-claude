Open Claude is a parody product by Scalia Studio.

It does one thing: it opens Claude. If the Claude desktop app is installed it opens that, otherwise it opens https://claude.ai in your browser.

It does not connect to the internet itself, collect data, run in the background or update itself. It installs for your user account only and can be removed from Settings > Apps.
